*   **Industry-Funded Studies:** Low weight. High risk of bias; identified as a source of systematic obfuscation.

**C. Correlated Evidence (Shared Methodological Assumptions)**
*   **Western Cohort Bias:** The Harvard/Nurses' Health Study and NHANES/MESA share a reliance on Western dietary patterns. **E. Unresolved Issues**
