**The point:** We don't know the sign of the confounding bias. Assigning 0% true effect assumes we've solved the direction problem. We haven't.

2. We assume: LDL-C elevation causes CVD

**The problem:** We haven't *proven* that egg-induced LDL-C elevation causes CVD in the Chinese population. The inverse association could mean:
- LDL-C is not the causal mediator in this population
- Or: LDL-C elevation from eggs is offset by other protective components (choline, lutein, etc.)
- Or: The mechanistic pathway is population-specific

Dismissing the largest study because it contradicts our mechanistic assumption is **assumption-driven reasoning**, not evidence-driven reasoning.
