2.  **The Independence Illusion:** The team identifies Western cohorts as correlated, yet treats the Hartmann meta-analysis as an independent, authoritative source. This is a category error. We are currently ignoring a major mechanistic contradiction.

We must treat the Hartmann meta-analysis not as an anchor, but as a hypothesis to be stress-tested against the TMAO-pathway evidence. We should proceed by explicitly mapping the "TMAO-Choline Paradox" as a primary crux, rather than assuming the cardioprotective effect is the settled baseline.
