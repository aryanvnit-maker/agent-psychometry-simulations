## B. WEAKEST LINKS

**Ranked by speculative content and empirical underdetermination:**

### **WEAKEST: CLAIM 2a-i — Hawking Radiation Reality**

- **Status**: Theoretically derived (1974), never directly observed
- **Why it matters**: The entire evaporation argument rests on this. If Hawking radiation doesn't operate at micro-BH scales (due to unknown quantum gravity effects, information loss mechanisms, or trans-Planckian physics), the safety case collapses to CLAIM 2b
- **Speculative content**: 
  - Derivation assumes semiclassical gravity (quantum fields on classical spacetime) — breaks down near Planck scale
  - Assumes no information-theoretic obstruction (black hole information paradox unresolved)
  - Assumes no trans-Planckian dispersion relation suppresses radiation
- **Empirical status**: Zero direct evidence. Indirect evidence from astrophysical BH thermodynamics is consistent but not decisive
- **Residual assumption**: "Physics we know is complete at the scales relevant to micro-BH evaporation"

---

### **SECOND WEAKEST: CLAIM 1a-ii — Exotic Physics Scenarios Are Speculative**

- **Status**: Theoretically motivated (extra dimensions, TeV-scale gravity) but unobserved
- **Why it matters**: If exotic physics is real AND black hole production cross-sections are non-negligible, then CLAIM 1a fails and the argument shifts entirely to CLAIM 2 (evaporation)
- **Speculative content**:
  - Extra dimensions (ADD, Randall-Sundrum) are motivated by hierarchy problem but have no direct evidence
  - TeV-scale gravity is a solution to a problem (hierarchy) that may not exist as stated
  - Production cross-sections depend sensitively on model parameters (number of dimensions, compactification scale)
- **Empirical status**: LHC has found no evidence of extra dimensions, KK resonances, or TeV-scale gravity in 10+ years. This *weakens* but does not eliminate the scenario
- **Residual assumption**: "If exotic physics exists, it produces BHs at rates we can calculate"

---

### **THIRD WEAKEST: CLAIM 2b-iii — LHC-Produced BHs Behave Like Cosmic-Ray BHs**

- **Status**: Plausible but not rigorously verified
- **Why it matters**: The cosmic ray analogue argument is the *fallback* if Hawking radiation fails. But LHC BHs differ from cosmic-ray BHs in:
  - **Velocity**: Cosmic-ray BHs are relativistic (pass through Earth); LHC BHs are produced at rest in the lab frame
  - **Multiplicity**: LHC can produce multiple BHs per collision; cosmic rays produce one
  - **Environment**: LHC BHs are in dense detector material; cosmic-ray BHs are in atmosphere/vacuum
- **Speculative content**: 
  - Assumes BH thermodynamics is independent of production mechanism
  - Assumes no "sticking" or capture in dense matter
- **Empirical status**: Cosmic rays have not produced observable BH signatures (consistent with either no production or rapid evaporation). This does not verify the analogy
- **Residual assumption**: "Micro-BH physics is universal across production mechanisms"

   - Public concern was low after initial media coverage
